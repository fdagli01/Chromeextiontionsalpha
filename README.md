# 🗺️ Polyglot Chronicle (Anno MDVI)

*"Arquivo Real de Rotas & Palavras"*

**Polyglot Chronicle** is a meticulously crafted Chrome extension that transforms the mundane task of vocabulary building into an immersive journey through history, philosophy, and linguistics. Inspired by the *Casa da Índia*—the central organ of the Portuguese Empire's overseas administration—this tool serves as your personal digital archive for routes, words, and knowledge.

---

## 🎭 The Concept: A Learning Odyssey
Polyglot Chronicle treats language learning as a simulation. Instead of flat lists, you navigate through distinct temporal and cultural "mainframes":

*   🏛️ **Castra Aeterna (Rome):** Master Latin roots and classical vocabulary within the rigid, disciplined aesthetics of the Roman Empire.
*   🇫🇷 **La Terreur (French Revolution):** Experience the frantic and radical vocabulary of 18th-century France.
*   ⚓ **Casa da Índia (Portuguese Discovery):** Explore the Age of Discovery through the lexicon of the 16th-century Portuguese maritime empire.
*   🇷🇺 **K.G.B. Terminal (Soviet Era):** Engage with Russian language features in a high-stakes, clandestine atmosphere.

Every theme features **custom UI motifs, ambient soundscapes, and thematic trivia**, turning your vocabulary reviews into an archival investigation.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

*   **SM-2 Based SRS:** Intelligent scheduling for vocabulary reviews powered by the SM-2 algorithm to maximize memory retention.
*   **Struggling Tracker:** Tracks difficult words and prioritizes them until you achieve mastery.
*   **Curated Context & Enrichment:** Automatically backfills historical facts, philosophical notes, example sentences (with Turkish translations), and transliterations.
*   **Portable Backups:** Built-in export/import functionality ensures your vocabulary progress remains safe, independent of browser storage limitations.
*   **Gamified Learning:** Earn XP, track daily streaks, and unlock unique badges as you archive your knowledge.
*   **Immersive Audio:** Thematic background ambient soundscapes and auto-pronunciation features for a true deep-learning experience.
